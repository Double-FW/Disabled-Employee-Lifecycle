# Related public Double-FW guidance

The following repositories were confirmed public on 22 November 2026. They are optional specialist routes, not copied source bodies or dependencies on private materials. Record the exact revision and sections actually used. A public link does not establish inspection, source correctness, law, policy adoption or permission to reproduce the work.

| Resource | Relevant use |
| --- | --- |
| [Terminology and Principles](https://github.com/Double-FW/Terminology-and-Principles) | Shared meanings, authority, evidence and review status. |
| [Inclusion for managers and HR systems](https://github.com/Double-FW/Inclusion-for-Managers-and-HR-Systems) | Detailed recruitment, support, performance, transition and exit workflows, decision routes and confidential blank structures. |
| [Comparative task analysis](https://github.com/Double-FW/Task-Analysis-for-Comparative-Accessibility-Evaluation-of-Internal-Systems) | Investigate complete work outcomes, context, assistance and cumulative burden; do not rate employee capability. |
| [QA accessibility testing](https://github.com/Double-FW/QA_Accessibility_Testing) | Select scoped technical outcomes, procedures and evidence; a mapping or automated pass is not overall conformance. |
| [Data ethics and disability](https://github.com/Double-FW/Data_Ethics_and_Disability) | Participation, evidence handling, privacy and responsible use of group data. |
| [Cognitive UX design](https://github.com/Double-FW/Cognitive-UX-Design) | Clarity, memory demands, navigation and choice; preserve named source identifiers and avoid diagnostic prescriptions. |
| [Accessibility programme runbooks](https://github.com/Double-FW/Accessibility_Programme_Runbooks) | Local planning, delivery responsibilities, improvement and review. |
| [Inclusion and accessibility policy](https://github.com/Double-FW/Inclusion-and-Accessibility-Policy) | Distinguish proposed commitments, actual adoption, verified practice and legal-applicability questions. |
| [EU AI Act accessibility and delivery](https://github.com/Double-FW/EU-AI-Act-accessibility-disability-rights-and-delivery) | Secondary governance orientation where relevant; establish legal scope and current obligations from primary sources. |

The HR knowledge edition supplies practical detail; this repository retains the six-phase strategic frame. The HR edition’s [lifecycle crosswalk](https://github.com/Double-FW/Inclusion-for-Managers-and-HR-Systems/blob/main/guidance/lifecycle-source-crosswalk.md) records a bounded review of an earlier lifecycle revision. Its historical source checks and qualifications do not automatically update when this repository changes; inspect the revision cited before using that record as a statement about current content.

If a linked repository becomes unavailable, state the missing evidence, continue independent authorised work from maintained local guidance and do not invent the linked contents. Preserve specialist meanings and source status. Follow [evidence boundaries](Evidence_and_Decision_Boundaries.md) and [NOTICE](NOTICE.md) before importing external content or acting on a reference.
