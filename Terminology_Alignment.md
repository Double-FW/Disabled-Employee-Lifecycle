# Terminology alignment

Use [Double-FW Terminology and Principles](https://github.com/Double-FW/Terminology-and-Principles), inspected at revision `219b5feddd271bcdf70ea0ac0c4bc5c62fe9a0f4`. Its [governance](https://github.com/Double-FW/Terminology-and-Principles/blob/219b5feddd271bcdf70ea0ac0c4bc5c62fe9a0f4/GOVERNANCE.md) and [decision register](https://github.com/Double-FW/Terminology-and-Principles/blob/219b5feddd271bcdf70ea0ac0c4bc5c62fe9a0f4/review/DECISIONS.md) distinguish scoped approved resolutions from proposed individual definitions. Alignment here is not blanket adoption of every glossary entry or an employer’s policy.

| Distinction | Application across the lifecycle |
| --- | --- |
| Accessibility and inclusion | Investigate barriers to meaningful participation and opportunity, not just technical availability or a compliance claim. |
| Task completion and task time | Check correct outcomes, quality, assistance and effort in context; a click, fast result or successful workaround does not establish independence. |
| User choice and preferences | Offer relevant usable alternatives without inferring a diagnosis or forcing disclosure. A declined option is not proof that need has ended. |
| Content consumption and engagement | Investigate access to meaning and chosen participation; longer engagement may reflect difficulty. |
| Equivalent/comparable experience (D02) | State comparison across outcomes, effort, privacy, availability, control and dignity; identical interfaces or times are not the goal. |
| Satisfactory and satisfaction (D01) | Use Satisfactory for the proposed experience principle; satisfaction is a person’s assessment. Ratings do not waive unmet requirements. |
| Emotional lenses (D03) | Feeling Seen, Feeling Capable, Feeling Confident, Feeling Safe and Feeling Valued are intended qualities and research lenses, not feelings assigned from a technical pass. |

Use named Cognitive UX `CP-01`–`CP-10` references with their owning source (D04). W3C ACT refers to the actual W3C work and rule status; a local ACT-style procedural test and user-oriented task script have different scopes (D05). WCAG robustness, Agentic Robustness and a particular system’s reliability are distinct; Feeling Safe does not prove security or interoperability (D06).

Keep observations, participant accounts, measurements, estimates, hypotheses, synthetic examples, proposals and approvals distinct (D07). `unknown`, `not_tested` and `not_applicable` have different meanings. Unresolved is an issue/decision state; task-blocked and an actual human decision are separate. Version metadata, a completed template or a repository merge is not approval or evidence that a test or referral occurred.

Use adopted Double-FW meanings for its own methods while preserving the source-owned meanings and applicability of laws, standards and contracts (D08). Required local practice, an external obligation and an intended experience outcome are not interchangeable. Do not relabel a locally prohibited action as universally unlawful without a proper basis.

Respect identity and language preferences, including Deaf and neurodivergent identities. Do not infer capability, clinical status, needs, strengths, consent or emotion from group membership or behaviour. The opinion article’s quoted AI statements retain their historical source status and do not override these conventions.
