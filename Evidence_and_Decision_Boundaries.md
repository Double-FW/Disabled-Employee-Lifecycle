# Evidence and decision boundaries

Use this with the [main guide](Accessibility%20in%20the%20Employee%20Lifecycle.md) and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). This is proposed practitioner guidance, not adopted policy, legal advice, a participant study or an implemented control system.

## Interpret the source correctly

The lifecycle narrative provides a design framework and intended outcomes, not a validated intervention or quantified benefit model. Its benefits require appropriate evidence and attribution. The companion opinion article includes a historical, edited AI conversation. A model’s self-description does not establish measured bias, training-data composition, actual product access or the behaviour of another deployment. Preserve opinion/transcript status, publication date, source identity and qualifications with excerpts. Do not turn an illustrative quotation or proposed answer into ground truth.

## Evidence, results and decisions

| Item | What it establishes within scope |
| --- | --- |
| Observation | What was actually seen in the stated context; it does not necessarily establish cause or population effects. |
| Participant report | The reported experience, with context and limits; do not dismiss it because direct observation is unavailable. |
| Measurement or estimate | The stated quantity with units, method, sample/period and uncertainty; estimates are not observations. |
| Hypothesis or intended benefit | A question or proposed relationship to investigate, not an outcome already achieved. |
| Synthetic example or blank template | An illustration or drafting aid, not employee evidence or a real action. |
| Human decision | An actual authorised decision with scope, basis and date where supplied; not inferred from generated text. |

For a specified check, use `pass`, `fail`, `unknown`, `not_tested` or `not_applicable`. A pass supports only that check, artefact, environment and scope. Unknown means the evidence has not established the result; not tested means the evaluation did not run; not applicable requires a rationale. Open decisions, task outcomes, legal applicability and human acceptance are separate fields.

Keep mandatory blockers and non-time burdens visible. Do not assign blocked work zero successful-completion time, convert a reported delay into observed data, or generalise small samples. For group rates, state denominators, missingness and uncertainty without forcing identity disclosure or involuntary monitoring. Consider re-identification risk even after names are removed or results aggregated.

## Support, authority and privacy

Ask about the actual task, practical needs and choices rather than assuming a condition determines support. Ordinary support should not wait for unnecessary disclosure or paperwork. A distinct formal, safety or health-related process may need proportionate information and appropriate advice; do not promise that such information can never be relevant.

Practical agreement about sharing is not by itself a complete data-processing justification. For actual worker health information, establish purpose, applicable legal basis/conditions, minimisation, access, security, retention, notice and review through authorised expertise and current primary sources. Do not install a universal retention period or promise that consent withdrawal recalls existing records. No evidence store, monitoring system, escalation service, human owner or secure redaction mechanism is configured by this repository.

Completed records belong only in approved confidential systems. Keep employee/applicant, health, passport, grievance and performance information out of public repositories and training datasets. Do not infer diagnosis, personality, capability, strengths or consent from group membership, behaviour or missing information.

Drafting a plan or referral does not authorise sending it, accessing live records, changing systems or making an employment decision. Stop an affected unsafe/unauthorised action, identify the human decision needed and continue independent reversible work. Do not block urgent interim support simply because a non-essential administrative field is missing.

## Current primary-source routes

The following routes were checked for editorial orientation on 22 November 2026. This is not a full legal review, completeness claim or determination for any employer or case. Establish current text, commencement, actual jurisdiction and applicability before relying on a legal statement.

- [GOV.UK Equality Act 2010 guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/equality-act-2010-guidance) identifies its England, Scotland and Wales scope and provides a separate Northern Ireland route. Do not treat a general UK label as a single equality-law framework.
- [ICO worker health information guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/information-about-workers-health/) is the primary regulator’s starting route for health-data handling. The inspected ICO text distinguishes lawful basis and additional health-data conditions and is marked under review following legislative changes. Recheck the current guidance for a real decision; an archived or indexed page is not a currency guarantee.

The [AbilityNet article on AI and disabled job applicants](https://abilitynet.org.uk/news-blogs/Is-AI-discriminating-against-disabled-job-applicants), linked by the opinion article, was accessible during this editorial check. It is a commentary/interview resource, not independent verification of every quoted model claim or a particular recruitment deployment.

## Retrieval and training preparation

Keep maintained guidance, agent conduct, source status, rights and terminology together. Do not silently truncate a rule, phase or evidence qualification. If ingesting the opinion article, retain its content type and limitations with every relevant excerpt or keep it in a distinct source class; do not merge the transcript into factual HR advice or evidence of executed decisions.

A later dataset/export needs an explicit inventory of substantive content and exclusions, source revision, paths and rights, plus appropriate privacy and duplication review. Linked repository content is not automatically included. No retrieval service, model, exporter or Hugging Face sync is installed by these documents.
